Privacy Policy

App Name: QR & Barcode Scanner

QR and Barcode Scanner is designed to scan, create, save, and manage QR codes and barcodes directly on your device. Your scanned results, generated QR codes and barcodes, and history are processed and stored locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the app uses permissions, handles your data, and interacts with third-party services.

1. Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

Words with capitalized initial letters have specific meanings defined under the following conditions. These definitions shall have the same meaning regardless of whether they appear in singular or in plural.

Definitions

  • Application or Service refers to QR & Barcode Scanner, the software program provided by us.
  • You means the individual accessing or using the Service.
  • Personal Data means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Usage Data means data collected automatically, either generated by the use of the Service or from the Service infrastructure itself (e.g., ad impressions, crash logs).
  • Local Data refers to history records, settings, and generated images/files created within the App and stored strictly on the user's physical device storage.

2. Collecting and Using Your Data

Local Data Processing & Scanning: The App functions purely as an on-device utility. All QR code and barcode scanning, generation, and history storage take place locally on your device. No scanned data, generated codes, or personal content leave your device or are collected by us.

  • Scan & Create: Enables scanning and creation of standard QR codes and 1D/2D barcodes. When scanning or generating codes that contain personal data formats (such as email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or URLs), these data types are processed strictly in memory on your device (ephemerally) to perform app functionality. They are never transmitted, saved, or collected on external servers.
  • Scan & Create History: History records are stored locally on your physical device. You can disable, clear, or export (TXT/CSV) your history at any time through the app settings.
  • No Server Storage: We do not collect, receive, or store any of your scanned or generated codes—including email addresses or phone numbers contained within them—on external servers.

3. App Permissions and How They Are Used

To provide core scanning and file saving capabilities, the App requests specific device permissions. You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

1. Camera Access

Purpose: Required to capture live video feeds so the App can scan QR codes and barcodes in real-time using Camera.

Privacy: The camera feed is processed exclusively in real-time on your device memory. No photos or video recordings are secretly taken, saved, or transmitted to any server.

2. Device Storage & System Pickers (No Sensitive Media Permissions Required)

Our app uses Android's modern, secure System Frameworks to handle images and export files without requiring broad storage permissions:

  • Scanning Images from Gallery: When you choose to scan a barcode from an existing photo, Android launches its native Photo Picker interface. You select the specific image you want to scan. The App only accesses the exact image file you explicitly select.
  • Saving Generated Barcodes & Reports: When exporting generated QR images or scan results (PDF / TXT), the App utilizes Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF). The system file chooser prompts you to choose where to save the file on your device.

3. Network & WiFi State

Purpose: Required to display advertisements, check for app updates, load Remote Config settings, collect crash reports and diagnostic information, and check network connectivity before opening scanned web links.

4. Vibration

Purpose: Provides subtle haptic feedback (vibration) when a QR code or barcode is successfully recognized.

5. Advertising ID

Purpose: Used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to deliver advertisements and analyze ad performance in compliance with Google Play Policies.

4. User Consent for Permissions

All sensitive permissions (such as Camera access) require your explicit consent before they are activated. You can deny permission requests; however, denying Camera access will prevent the live scanning feature from working (scanning via imported gallery images will remain available).

5. Feedback-Related Device Information

When you choose to send feedback through the app (e.g., via email), we may collect certain non-personal device information to help troubleshoot issues and improve your experience. This information is only included if you manually send a feedback message from the app.

The feedback email may include:

  • App version and build number
  • Device model (e.g., Samsung SM-A125F)
  • Android version (e.g., Android 11)
  • Screen resolution and screen density (DPI)
  • Available RAM and memory info
  • Language and locale settings (e.g., en_US)
  • Time zone (e.g., London/Europe)
  • This information is only shared if you voluntarily choose to send it via email.
  • We do not store, track, or sell this data. It is used solely for debugging and support purposes based on your request.

6. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To provide better functionality, improve user experience, support app performance monitoring, and enable monetization, our app uses third-party services and Software Development Kits (SDKs). These services may process data according to their own privacy policies. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • Ad interaction data (such as impressions, clicks, and frequency)
  • General app usage and performance data (such as session duration and crash-related data)

Ad Personalization: AdMob may serve either personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your consent and device settings. You can opt out via Device Settings or Google Ads Settings. AdMob Privacy Policy

Firebase (Google)

We use Firebase services to improve app performance, stability, and user experience.

  • Firebase Analytics: Used to collect usage and technical info to understand app usage. (Analytics Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect crash reports and diagnostic info to fix errors. (Crashlytics Privacy Policy)

In-App Updates & Reviews

Our App uses Google Play In-App Updates and the In-App Review API. Google Play may process device metadata, app version, and installation metrics in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data Safety Summary

  • QR/barcode content collected by us: None
  • App activity: Yes (analytics via Firebase)
  • Device or IDs: Yes (AdMob advertising ID)
  • Crash logs: Yes (Firebase Crashlytics)

We do not upload scanned images, generated QR/barcode images, or local history to servers operated by us.

8. Data Retention Policy

  • Local Data: Scan and creation history is stored locally while history recording is enabled. You can disable history recording or clear history using the App's Settings, or by clearing app data/uninstalling.
  • Exported Files: Any PDF, PNG, or TXT files created using the file exporter reside in your local device directories until you explicitly delete them.

Network connectivity is used strictly for ads, analytics, crash reporting, updates, and configurations. We do not intentionally upload your contents or files to servers operated by us.
